### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Connect** – 联系 / 沟通 (They help you connect)
* **Understand** – 理解 / 听懂 (The goal is to understand and to be understood)
* **Communicate** – 传达 / 交流 (You communicated your need)
* **Achieve** – 实现 / 达到 (You achieved your goal)
* **Simplify** – 简化 (So simplify it. Make it easy.)
* **Pretend** – 假装 (The most important rule is do not pretend)
* **Repeat** – 重复 (Could you please repeat that?)
* **Describe** – 描述 (Describe the object)
* **Troubleshoot** – 故障排除 / 解决问题 (Troubleshoot calmly)

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Deep dive** – 深度探索
* **Connection, not perfection** – 重在沟通，而非完美
* **Keep the ball in play** – 让球保持在比赛状态 / 保持对话继续
* **Ask back technique** – 反问技巧
* **A shared reality** – 共同的现实 / 共同经历的事
* **Current events** – 时事
* **Active listening** – 积极倾听
* **Non-verbal communication** – 非语言交流
* **Verbal fillers** – 语气词 / 填充词
* **Key takeaways** – 核心要点
