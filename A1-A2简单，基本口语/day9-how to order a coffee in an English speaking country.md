### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Freeze** – 僵住 / 呆住 (They walk into a busy coffee shop... and they just freeze)
* **Avoid** – 避免 (One phrase we really must avoid)
* **Demand** – 要求 / 强求 (It is a demand, not a request)
* **Blend** – 融合 / 连读 (That D sound at the end blends right into the next word)
* **Customize** – 客制化 / 定制 (You have customized your drink)
* **Tap** – 拍卡支付 / 轻触 (Can I tap?)
* **Catch** – 捕捉 / 听清 (Sorry, I didn't catch that)
* **Crave** – 极度渴望 (getting a craving - *衍生动词*)

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Deep dive** – 深度探索
* **Threshold anxiety** – 门槛焦虑
* **Safety net** – 安全网 / 后备方案
* **I'd like / Can I have...** – 我想要 / 能给我...吗
* **Speed round** – 快问快答环节
* **For here or to go** – 在这吃还是带走 (英式: eat in or take away)
* **Is this seat taken?** – 这个座位有人吗？
* **Could you please repeat that?** – 能请您重复一遍吗？
* **I didn't catch that.** – 我没听清。
