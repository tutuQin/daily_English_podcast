### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Unpack** – 拆解 / 分析 (Can we unpack that?)
* **Freeze** – 僵住 / 呆住 (You freeze. You stop.)
* **Translate** – 翻译 (Translate it directly into English)
* **Swap** – 交换 / 替换 (You swap one word for another word)
* **Immerse** – 沉浸 (You are immersed in English)
* **Mimic** – 模仿 (To mimic means to copy)
* **Validate** – 肯定 / 认可 (You validate them first)
* **Reinforce** – 强化 / 巩固 (It reinforces the memory)

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Daily use sentences** – 日常用语
* **The translation trap** – 翻译陷阱
* **Word for word** – 逐字地
* **A state of being** – 存在状态
* **The mother of skill** – 技能之母 (Repetition is the mother of skill)
* **Social harmony** – 社会和谐
* **Spaced repetition** – 间隔重复
* **Memory hook** – 记忆钩子 / 记忆点
* **Long-term memory** – 长期记忆
* **Take action** – 采取行动
