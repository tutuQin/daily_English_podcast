# Day 3: Shopping Mall Conversation

A: Hello everyone, welcome to the English podcast journey.

> 大家好，欢迎来到英语播客之旅。

B: Uh, it is really wonderful to have you here with us today. I am very happy to be back.

> 呃，今天能有你们在这里和我们在一起真是太棒了。我很高兴能回来。

A: Yes, and today, we have a very, um, exciting destination for our deep dive. We are not going to a classroom today.

> 是的，今天，我们有一个非常，嗯，令人兴奋的深度探索目的地。我们今天不去教室。

B: Right. We are not going to a library either. Today we are going to the shopping mall. The shopping mall, yes. This is actually one of my favorite places.

> 对。我们也不去图书馆。今天我们要去购物中心。购物中心，是的。这其实是我最喜欢的地方之一。

A: And do you know what?

> 你知道吗？

B: What?

> 什么？

A: It is a perfect place to practice your English. That is an interesting idea. Usually when we think of learning English, well, we think of books. Yeah, exactly. Books and tests. So why is the mall so good for learning?

> 这是一个练习英语的完美地方。这是一个有趣的想法。通常当我们想到学习英语时，嗯，我们会想到书。是的，完全正确。书和考试。那么为什么购物中心这么适合学习呢？

B: Well think about it. The mall is full of opportunities. It is a real place, with real people. You have three main ways to practice there. Three ways. Okay, can you explain them to us?

> 嗯，想想看。购物中心充满了机会。这是一个真实的地方，有真实的人。你在那里有三种主要的练习方式。三种方式。好的，你能给我们解释一下吗？

A: Of course. First, you can speak with people. There are workers in the stores, and they are there to talk to you, they are paid to be friendly. That is very true. It is their job to help us. Second, you can read. There are signs everywhere in a mall.

> 当然。首先，你可以和人交谈。商店里有工作人员，他们在那里就是为了和你说话的，他们拿了报酬就会表现得很友好。这是非常真实的。帮助我们是他们的工作。第二，你可以阅读。购物中心里到处都是标志。

B: Right, like signs for the stores.

> 对，比如商店的标志。

A: Yes, and you can read signs for prices, you can read signs for sales, you can read signs for directions. It is like a big reading book, but you can walk inside it. I really like that image, walking inside a book.

> 是的，你可以阅读价格标志，你可以阅读促销标志，你可以阅读指示牌。它就像一本大阅读书，但你可以走在里面。我真的很喜欢那个画面，走进一本书里。

B: And um, what is the third way?

> 那么，嗯，第三种方式是什么？

A: The third way is listening. Listening to the people.

> 第三种方式是倾听。听人们说话。

B: Yes. You can listen to interactions. You can hear how other people buy things. You can hear how they order food.

> 是的。你可以听互动。你可以听到其他人是如何买东西的。你可以听到他们是如何点餐的。

A: Oh, I see. It is real-world English. It is not English from a textbook. It is English from life. That sounds great. So today is a practical guide. We will learn useful phrases for shopping and for eating.

> 哦，我明白了。这是真实世界的英语。它不是教科书上的英语。它是生活中的英语。听起来很棒。所以今天是一份实用指南。我们将学习购物和就餐的有用短语。

B: Exactly. We want you to feel comfortable, we want you to feel ready to shop, we want you to enjoy the experience. So let's start at the beginning. Imagine you are walking into a store.

> 完全正确。我们希望你感到舒适，我们希望你觉得准备好购物了，我们希望你享受这个体验。让我们从头开始。想象一下你正走进一家商店。

A: Okay. Maybe it is a clothing store. It is bright. There is music playing. You walk through the door. What happens first?

> 好的。也许是一家服装店。里面很明亮。播放着音乐。你走过这扇门。首先会发生什么？

B: Usually a store clerk will see you. A clerk?

> 通常店员会看到你。店员？

A: Yes, a clerk is the person who works in the store. They want to be friendly. So they will greet you. They will say hello.

> 是的，店员就是在商店里工作的人。他们想要表现得友好。所以他们会跟你打招呼。他们会说你好。

B: And what do they usually say after hello?

> 那么他们通常在打完招呼后会说什么呢？

A: They will likely ask, "Can I help you?" "Can I help you?"

> 他们可能会问：“我能帮您吗？”“我能帮您吗？”

B: Yes. It is a very simple question. They are asking if you need assistance. They want to know if you are looking for something specific.

> 是的。这是一个非常简单的问题。他们在问你是否需要帮助。他们想知道你是否在寻找什么特定的东西。

A: Okay, so I hear the question "Can I help you?". How should I respond?

> 好的，那么我听到了“我能帮您吗？”这个问题。我应该怎么回答？

B: Well, it depends on your situation. We have two scenarios here. Scenario A is when you know what you want. You have a plan.

> 嗯，这取决于你的情况。我们这里有两种情景。情景 A 是当你知道自己想要什么时。你有一个计划。

A: Okay, let's say I need a new jacket. I know I need a jacket. I do not want to waste time. In that case, you can use the phrase, "I am looking for..." "I am looking for."

> 好的，假设我需要一件新夹克。我知道我需要一件夹克。我不想浪费时间。在那种情况下，你可以使用这个短语：“我在找……”“我在找。”

B: Exactly. So you would say, "I am looking for a jacket." What if I need shoes?

> 完全正确。所以你可以说：“我在找一件夹克。”如果我需要鞋子怎么办？

A: Then you say, "I am looking for shoes."

> 那你可以说：“我在找鞋子。”

B: And what if I need a gift for my friend?

> 那如果我需要给我朋友买个礼物呢？

A: You could say, "I am looking for a gift." It is a very useful structure. "I am looking for" plus the item you need. That is very clear. It tells the clerk exactly what you need.

> 你可以说：“我在找一份礼物。”这是一个非常有用的结构。“我在找”加上你需要的东西。这非常清楚。它准确地告诉了店员你需要什么。

B: But let's talk about Scenario B.

> 但让我们谈谈情景 B。

A: Yes, Scenario B is very common. This is when you do not have a specific plan. You are just browsing. Browsing? What does browsing mean?

> 是的，情景 B 非常常见。这是当你没有一个具体的计划时。你只是在随便看看。浏览（随便看看）？随便看看是什么意思？

B: Browsing means you are walking around and looking at things. Yeah. But you are not sure if you want to buy anything yet. You are just looking.

> 随便看看意味着你四处走动，看看东西。是的。但你还不确定是否想买任何东西。你只是看看。

A: Right. Sometimes I just want to walk around. I do not want the clerk to follow me. I just want to look quietly. In this situation, when the clerk asks "Can I help you?", you can say this magic phrase: "I am just looking." "I am just looking."

> 对。有时候我只是想四处走走。我不想店员跟着我。我只是想安静地看看。在这种情况下，当店员问“我能帮您吗？”时，你可以说这句神奇的短语：“我只是随便看看。”“我只是随便看看。”

B: Yes. "I am just looking." This is a polite way to say, "No thank you, I am happy looking alone." Is it rude to say this? I worry about being rude.

> 是的。“我只是随便看看。”这是一种礼貌的表达方式，意思是“不，谢谢你，我很高兴一个人看看。”这么说会不礼貌吗？我担心会显得粗鲁。

A: No, not at all. It is very polite. It is a normal thing to say in a store. I like that. It takes the pressure off. I can relax. It really does. When you say "I am just looking," the clerk will usually say, "Okay, let me know if you need anything," and then they will leave you alone. It is very comfortable.

> 不，一点也不会。这非常有礼貌。这是在商店里一句很平常的话。我喜欢这样。它减轻了压力。我可以放松了。确实如此。当你说“我只是随便看看”时，店员通常会说，“好的，如果您需要什么请告诉我”，然后他们就不会打扰你了。这让人感觉很舒服。

B: Okay, so we are in the store. We are looking around. We use the phrase, "I'm just looking."

> 好的，现在我们在商店里了。我们在四处看。我们使用短语“我只是随便看看”。

A: And then I see something I like. Great. You found something interesting.

> 然后我看到了一件我喜欢的东西。太好了。你找到了一些有趣的东西。

B: Yes, I found a nice shirt. It is blue. It looks soft.

> 是的，我找到了一件好看的衬衫。它是蓝色的。看起来很柔软。

A: But there is a problem.

> 但有个问题。

B: Oh?

> 哦？

A: I cannot find the price tag. I do not know how much money it costs. This happens all the time. Sometimes the tag falls off, sometimes it is hidden. You need to ask about the cost. What is the best question to ask the clerk?

> 我找不到价格标签。我不知道它要多少钱。这种事经常发生。有时标签掉了，有时它被藏起来了。你需要询问价格。问店员最好的问题是什么？

B: You can simply say, "How much is this?" "How much is this?" Short and simple. "How much is this?" Is there another way to say it? Maybe a little longer?

> 你可以简单地说：“这个多少钱？”“这个多少钱？”简短明了。“这个多少钱？”有其他说法吗？也许稍微长一点？

A: Yes. You can also say, "How much does this cost?" "How much does this cost?" Both are perfect. They mean exactly the same thing. You can use whichever one is easier for you to remember. Let's practice them again. "How much is this?"

> 有的。你也可以说：“这个要花多少钱？”“这个要花多少钱？”两者都很完美。它们的意思完全一样。你可以用你觉得比较容易记住的那个。让我们再练习一遍。“这个多少钱？”

B: "How much is this?" "How much does this cost?" "How much does this cost?"

> “这个多少钱？”“这个要花多少钱？”“这个要花多少钱？”

A: Okay, I have the price now.

> 好的，我现在知道价格了。

B: But I love to save money. I love a good deal. We all love a good deal. Saving money is a good feeling. Sometimes stores have lower prices for a short time. This is called a sale. A sale. I see signs for sales sometimes. They are usually red signs with big white letters.

> 但我喜欢省钱。我喜欢划算的买卖。我们都喜欢划算的买卖。省钱的感觉很好。有时商店会在短时间内降低价格。这叫做促销（打折）。促销。我有时会看到促销的牌子。它们通常是带有白色大字的红牌子。

A: Yes, exactly. Red is a very common color for sales. If you want to know if the item has a special lower price, you can ask a question. What question should I ask?

> 是的，没错。红色是促销非常常见的颜色。如果你想知道这件商品是否有特别优惠的价格，你可以问个问题。我应该问什么问题？

B: You can ask, "Is this on sale?" "Is this on sale?" It is a really good question to ask. Maybe the price is lower than you think. Maybe there is a special promotion. Can I ask about discounts generally? Maybe the store has a coupon.

> 你可以问：“这个打折吗？”“这个打折吗？”这是一个非常好的问题。也许价格比你想象的还要低。也许有特别的促销活动。我可以一般性地询问折扣吗？也许店里有优惠券。

A: Yes, you can. You can ask, "Do you have any discounts today?" "Do you have any discounts today?" Do not be afraid to ask this. The worst thing they can say is no.

> 是的，你可以。你可以问：“你们今天有什么折扣吗？”“你们今天有什么折扣吗？”不要害怕问这个问题。他们能说的最坏的结果就是没有。

B: But often, they might say, "Yes, actually. If you buy two shirts, you get a discount." That is great advice. Never be afraid to ask. It helps you practice your English and it might save you money.

> 但通常，他们可能会说：“是的，其实有。如果你买两件衬衫，你就能得到一个折扣。”这是很好的建议。永远不要害怕提问。这能帮助你练习英语，而且还能为你省钱。

A: Exactly. It is a win-win situation.

> 完全正确。这是一个双赢的情况。

B: Okay, so I found the shirt. I know the price. Now, I want to see if it fits my body. I want to put it on. This is a very important step. You should not buy clothes without trying them on. Sizes can be different in every store.

> 好的，那么我找到了这件衬衫。我知道了价格。现在，我想看看它是否合身。我想穿上它试试。这是一个非常重要的步骤。你不应该不试穿就买衣服。每家店的尺码可能会有所不同。

A: How do I ask the clerk for permission to wear it?

> 我该如何请求店员允许我试穿呢？

B: You use the phrase, "Can I try this on?" "Can I try this on?"

> 你可以使用这个短语：“我可以试穿这个吗？”“我可以试穿这个吗？”

A: Yes. "Try on" is a phrasal verb. It means to put clothes on your body to test the size. "Can I try this on?"

> 是的。“试穿”是一个动词短语。它的意思是把衣服穿在身上以测试尺码。“我可以试穿这个吗？”

B: And where do I go to do this? I cannot change my shirt in the middle of the store.

> 那我该去哪里试穿呢？我不能在商店中间换衬衫。

A: No, definitely not. You need to go to a special room. These are called fitting rooms. Fitting rooms. Or sometimes they are called dressing rooms.

> 不，绝对不行。你需要去一个专门的房间。这些被称为试衣间。试衣间。或者有时候它们被称为更衣室。

B: Dressing rooms. Are they different?

> 更衣室。它们有什么不同吗？

A: No, they are the same place. Fitting room or dressing room. It is a private room with a mirror and a door. Or maybe a curtain.

> 不，它们是同一个地方。试衣间或更衣室。这是一个有镜子和门的私密房间。或者可能是一个帘子。

B: Okay. So I ask, "Can I try this on?" and the clerk will show me where the fitting rooms are. Correct. Now, let's imagine you are shopping with a friend. Shopping with a friend is fun.

> 好的。所以我问“我可以试穿这个吗？”，然后店员就会指给我试衣间在哪里。正确。现在，让我们想象一下你和一个朋友在购物。和朋友购物很有趣。

A: Yes, I love shopping with friends. And it is a great opportunity to practice English conversation.

> 是的，我喜欢和朋友一起购物。而且这是一个练习英语对话的绝佳机会。

B: Yes. Okay, I come out of the fitting room. I am wearing the new shirt. I look in the mirror, but I want my friend's opinion. You can ask your friend, "What do you think of this?" "What do you think of this?" This asks for their general feeling. Do they like it? Do they hate it?

> 是的。好的，我从试衣间出来。我穿着这件新衬衫。我照着镜子，但我想要我朋友的意见。你可以问你的朋友：“你觉得这个怎么样？”“你觉得这个怎么样？”这是在询问他们总体的感觉。他们喜欢吗？他们讨厌吗？

A: What if I want to know if it looks good on my body?

> 如果我想知道这件衣服穿在我身上好不好看怎么办？

B: Then you can be more specific. You can ask, "Does this look good on me?" "Does this look good on me?" That is a brave question. It is. You have to be ready for an honest answer. Your friend might say yes, or they might say, "Hmm, not really."

> 那你可以更具体一点。你可以问：“这个穿在我身上好看吗？”“这个穿在我身上好看吗？”这是一个勇敢的问题。确实。你必须准备好听实话。你的朋友可能会说是的，或者他们可能会说：“嗯，不怎么好看。”

A: What if the answer is bad? What if the shirt does not fit well?

> 如果答案是不好怎么办？如果这件衬衫不太合身怎么办？

B: Well, this happens often in the fitting room. Maybe the shirt is too tight. Maybe it is too loose. Or maybe you just do not like the color. So, I need to solve this problem. I need to ask the clerk for help again.

> 嗯，这在试衣间里经常发生。也许这件衬衫太紧了。也许它太松了。或者也许你只是不喜欢这个颜色。所以，我需要解决这个问题。我需要再次向店员寻求帮助。

A: Yes. Do not just put the shirt back. Ask for what you need. The clerk can bring you a different shirt. Let's say the shirt is too big. It is huge. It looks like a tent. Then you ask, "Do you have this in a smaller size?"

> 是的。不要只是把衬衫放回去。提出你的需求。店员可以给你拿一件不同的衬衫。假设这件衬衫太大了。非常大。看起来像个帐篷。那你可以问：“你们有这个小一号的吗？”

B: Yes. Smaller size.

> 是的。小一号的。

A: And what if it is too small? It is too tight. Then you ask, "Do you have this in a bigger size?" "Do you have this in a bigger size?" Perfect. These questions are lifesavers. They help you find the perfect fit. What if the size is okay, but I do not like the color? The shirt is yellow, but I look better in blue.

> 如果它太小了呢？它太紧了。那你可以问：“你们有这个大一号的吗？”“你们有这个大一号的吗？”完美。这些问题真是救星。它们能帮你找到最合身的。如果尺码没问题，但我不喜欢这个颜色怎么办？这件衬衫是黄色的，但我穿蓝色更好看。

B: Then you can ask about the color. You can ask, "Do you have this in a different color?" "Do you have this in a different color?" Simple and effective. The clerk can check the computer or look in the back room for you. Excellent. Okay, let's say we found the perfect shirt. The size is right, the color is beautiful, the price is good. I am happy, I am ready to buy it.

> 那你可以询问颜色。你可以问：“你们有这个其他颜色的吗？”“你们有这个其他颜色的吗？”简单有效。店员可以查电脑或者去后面的库房帮你找。太棒了。好的，假设我们找到了完美的衬衫。尺码合适，颜色漂亮，价格也公道。我很高兴，我准备买下它。

A: Then it is time to pay. You need to go to the checkout counter. The checkout counter. This is where the cash register is. This is where you pay money.

> 那么是时候付钱了。你需要去收银台。收银台。这是收银机所在的地方。这是你付钱的地方。

B: Yes. When you get to the counter, the cashier will ask you some standard questions. You need to be ready to answer quickly. I sometimes get nervous at the counter because it is fast. What is the first question?

> 是的。当你到了收银台，收银员会问你一些标准问题。你需要准备好快速回答。我有时在收银台会紧张，因为节奏很快。第一个问题是什么？

A: Usually, they ask about payment. They will say, "Will that be cash or card?" "Will that be cash or card?" Cash means paper money and coins. Card means a credit card or debit card. So I can just answer, "Card, please," or "Cash, please."

> 通常，他们会询问支付方式。他们会说：“请问是用现金还是刷卡？”“请问是用现金还是刷卡？”现金指的是纸币和硬币。卡指的是信用卡或借记卡。所以我可以直接回答：“刷卡，谢谢”或者“现金，谢谢”。

B: Exactly. Keep it short. You do not need a long sentence. "Card, please" is perfect. What is the second question?

> 完全正确。保持简短。你不需要长句子。“刷卡，谢谢”就很完美了。第二个问题是什么？

A: They will usually ask, "Do you need a bag?" "Do you need a bag?" Sometimes people bring their own bags now, to help the environment. So you can say, "Yes, please," if you want a bag. Or you can say, "No thank you," if you have your own. Very simple. "Yes, please" or "No, thank you."

> 他们通常会问：“您需要袋子吗？”“您需要袋子吗？”现在有时候人们为了环保会自带袋子。所以如果你需要袋子，你可以说：“是的，谢谢。”如果你有自己的袋子，你也可以说：“不用了，谢谢”。非常简单。“是的，谢谢”或“不用了，谢谢”。

> 在你付款后，收银员会给你一件非常重要的东西。我买的东西。

> 嗯，是的，他们会给你衬衫。但他们还会给你别的东西。一张纸。购物小票（收据）。啊，购物小票。上面有价格和日期。

> 是的。为什么购物小票这么重要？我通常直接就把它扔了。

> 哦，请不要把它扔掉。购物小票是你的购买凭证。它证明你那天在那家店买了这件商品。为什么我需要证明这个？

> 你退货时需要用到它。退货。跟我们说说这个吧。什么是退货？

> 退货就是你把商品带回商店。也许你回到家才发现衬衫上有个洞。或者也许你的丈夫或妻子不喜欢它。

B: Oh no. So I want to give the shirt back and get my money back.

## 购物中心英语｜核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

### 一、核心动词（必背）

- browse 随便看看，浏览
- try on 试穿
- fit 合适，合身
- cost 花费，价值
- return 退换（商品）
- order 点餐，订购
- save 节省
- prove 证明

### 二、高频短语

- look for 寻找
- just looking 随便看看
- how much is this 这个多少钱
- on sale 打折，特价
- any discounts 任何折扣
- a smaller/bigger size 小一号/大一号
- a different color 其他颜色
- cash or card 现金还是刷卡
- change my mind 改变主意
- enjoy your meal 祝您用餐愉快
- where are the restrooms 洗手间在哪里
- ATM nearby 附近的自动取款机

### 三、易混区分

- fitting room / dressing room：试衣间 / 更衣室（两者意思相同，通用）
- I want / I'd like：我要（听起来像命令，不够礼貌） / 我想要（更加温和礼貌，建议使用）
- cash / card：现金（纸币和硬币） / 银行卡（信用卡或借记卡）
