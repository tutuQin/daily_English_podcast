### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Unpack** – 拆解 / 分析 (Let us unpack this idea)
* **Shut down** – 停止运转 / 关闭 (Your brain just shuts down)
* **Absorb** – 吸收 (A relaxed brain absorbs new information)
* **Force** – 迫使 (you force your tongue and your lips to move)
* **Memorize** – 背诵 / 记住 (try to memorize... from a textbook)
* **Train** – 训练 (it does not train your mouth)
* **Copy** – 模仿 / 复制 (You must copy the actor perfectly)
* **Pause** – 暂停 (You press the pause button)

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Dusty textbook** – 布满灰尘的教科书
* **Muscle memory** – 肌肉记忆
* **Human nature** – 人的本性
* **I am just kidding** – 我只是开玩笑
* **No way** – 不可能 / 没门
* **Everyday slang** – 日常俚语
* **Training wheels** – 辅助轮 (起辅助/过渡作用的事物)
* **Pause and repeat trick** – 暂停和重复技巧
* **Language detective** – 语言侦探
* **Context clues** – 语境线索
* **Main idea** – 主要意思 / 核心思想
