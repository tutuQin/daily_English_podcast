### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Handle** – 应对 / 处理 (how we should handle these tired legs)
* **Trust** – 相信 / 信任 (trusting the journey can change everything)
* **Require** – 需要 / 要求 (perfection is not required)
* **Balance** – 保持平衡 (learn how to balance better)
* **Plant** – 种植 / 播种 (you must plant the seeds)
* **Celebrate** – 庆祝 (celebrate one small win)
* **Steal** – 偷窃 / 夺走 (comparison steals your happiness)
* **Compare** – 比较 (comparison is the thief of joy - *名词形式*)

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Hit a wall** – 碰壁 / 陷入困境
* **Trust the journey / process** – 相信这段旅程 / 相信这个过程
* **Take action** – 采取行动
* **Try your best** – 尽你最大的努力
* **A small victory / win** – 一个小小的胜利
* **Comparison is the thief of joy** – 比较是快乐的偷窃者
* **Open doors** – 打开大门 / 带来机遇
* **Ups and downs** – 高潮与低谷 / 顺境与逆境
