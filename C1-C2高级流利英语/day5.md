## 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

### 核心动词 (Core Verbs)
* **Navigate**: 应对/导航 (e.g., navigate demanding environments)
* **Mitigate**: 减轻 (e.g., mitigate decision fatigue)
* **Stifle**: 扼杀/压制 (e.g., stifle spontaneity)
* **Consolidate**: 巩固/总结 (e.g., consolidate what we've argued)
* **Encapsulate**: 概括/缩影 (e.g., encapsulate campus life)
* **Thrive**: 蓬勃发展/繁荣 (e.g., survive and thrive)
* **Converge**: 汇聚/达成共识 (e.g., where we absolutely converge)

### 高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)
* **Burning out**: 倦怠/耗尽 (burnout: 词名形式)
* **Scaffolding**: 脚手架 (比喻学习或生活的支撑框架)
* **Survival mode**: 生存模式
* **Decision fatigue**: 决策疲劳
* **Unsung heroes**: 幕后英雄/默默无闻的功臣
* **High-stakes**: 高风险的/至关重要的 (e.g., high-stakes academic environment)
* **Passive compliance**: 被动顺从
* **Means to an end**: 达到目的的手段
* **Cognitive offloading**: 认知卸载 (指通过常规习惯减少大脑负担)
* **Spontaneous grammar**: 自发的语法 (比喻生活中的随机性与灵感)
* **That works for me**: 那对我来说可以 (表示赞同/协调成功)
