### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Picture / Recount**: To imagine or describe an event. 
    > 想象 / 叙述（在脑海中勾勒画面或回忆细节）
* **Disconnect / Untether**: To break the connection from something (like work). 
    > 断开连接 / 解绑（从工作压力或电子设备中抽离）
* **Mandate / Enforce**: To officially require or compel something to happen. 
    > 规定 / 执行（强制执行某项规则，如无闹钟或家庭时光）
* **Recharge / Restore**: To regain energy or return to a former state. 
    > 充电 / 恢复（通过休息找回精力和认知能力）
* **Commodify / Perform**: To turn something into a product or to act it out. 
    > 商品化 / 表演（将休息视为任务产出或演给别人看）
* **Equalize / Balance**: To make stable or consistent. 
    > 平衡 / 均等（消除压力差，达到心理平衡）

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Blank canvas**: An empty unstructured space for creativity or existence. 
    > 空白画布（比喻毫无规划、自由随性的周末）
* **Factory floor**: A highly structured environment for output and efficiency. 
    > 工厂车间（比喻像管理工作一样管理休息）
* **Airlock (Submarine)**: A transition space to equalize pressure. 
    > 气闸（比喻从高压工作过渡到放松状态的仪式）
* **Decision fatigue**: The tiredness resulting from making too many choices. 
    > 决策疲劳（因琐碎选择过多而导致的大脑疲惫）
* **Sunday Scaries**: The dread or anxiety felt before the workweek begins. 
    > 周日恐惧症（周日下午开始对周一工作感到的焦虑）
* **Zeigarnik effect**: The tendency to remember uncompleted tasks more than completed ones. 
    > 蔡加尼克效应（大脑因挂念未完成的事而无法彻底放松）
* **Hidden labor**: Domestic work that often goes unrecognized or unpaid. 
    > 隐形劳动（看似休闲实则耗能的家务，如准备大餐）
* **Path of least resistance**: The easiest way to do something, often defaulting to bad habits. 
    > 阻力最小的路径（指人不加克制时往往倾向于刷手机等易行之事）
* **Utilitarian mindset**: Valuing something only for its usefulness or productivity. 
    > 功利主义心态（休息只是为了周一能更好地打工）
* **Active recovery**: Engaging in light activity to help recover from intense work. 
    > 主动恢复（通过做家务、运动等非静止方式缓解心理压力）
