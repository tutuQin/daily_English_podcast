### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Navigate** (v. 应对/导航): To find a way through a difficult situation.
    > 在困难的情况下找到出路。
* **Escalate** (v. 升级): To increase in intensity or severity.
    > 在强度或严重程度上增加。
* **Substitute** (v. 替换): To use something in place of another.
    > 用某物代替另一物。
* **Recruit** (v. 招募/寻求帮助): To get someone to help you.
    > 让某人帮助你。
* **Neutralize** (v. 抵消/中和): To make something ineffective or harmless.
    > 使某物失效或无害。
* **Mimic** (v. 模仿): To copy the way someone speaks or acts.
    > 模仿某人说话或行为的方式。
* **Leverage** (v. 利用): To use something to maximum advantage.
    > 最大限度地利用某物。

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Missing a step in the dark** (黑暗中踩空): A metaphor for the sudden panic of forgetting a word.
    > 比喻忘词时的突然恐慌。
* **Triage system** (分诊系统/分级处理): A strategic framework to handle problems by severity.
    > 根据严重程度处理问题的战略框架。
* **Cognitive load** (认知负荷): The total amount of mental effort being used in the working memory.
    > 工作记忆中使用的心理努力总量。
* **Quick pivot** (快速转换): A fast shift to a synonym to keep a conversation moving.
    > 快速切换到同义词以保持对话进行。
* **Word detective** (词汇侦探): Collaboratively describing a forgotten word with a partner.
    > 与伙伴合作描述一个忘记的词。
* **Emergency rip cord** (应急拉绳): A final strategy (like saying "anyway") to end a stuck topic.
    > 结束卡壳话题的最终策略（如说“总之”）。
* **Amygdala hijack** (杏仁核劫持): When panic shuts down the brain's rational thinking areas.
    > 当恐慌关闭大脑的理性思考区域时。
* **It's on the tip of my tongue** (就在我嘴边): A filler phrase used to buy time and lower stress.
    > 用来赢得时间并减轻压力的填充短语。
* **Break the fourth wall** (打破第四面墙): Dropping a role-play to discuss the learning process directly.
    > 放下角色扮演，直接讨论学习过程。
