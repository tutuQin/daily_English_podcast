### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Critique / Examine**: To analyze and provide feedback on something. 
    > 评论 / 审视（对某物进行分析并提供反馈）
* **Bypass / Overlook**: To go around or fail to notice something. 
    > 绕过 / 忽视（直接跳过或未能注意到某事）
* **Validate / Acknowledge**: To confirm that something is true or felt by someone. 
    > 认可 / 承认（证实某事的真实性或确认某人的感受）
* **Anchor / Ground**: To connect an abstract idea to a concrete reality. 
    > 锚定 / 落实（将抽象概念与具体现实联系起来）
* **Implement / Deploy**: To put a plan or tool into action. 
    > 执行 / 布置（将计划或工具付诸行动）
* **Restructure / Reorganize**: To change the order or layout of something. 
    > 重构 / 重组（改变某物的顺序或布局）

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Comparison trap**: The habit of feeling bad by comparing oneself to others. 
    > 比较陷阱（通过与他人对比而产生负面情绪的习惯）
* **Tip of the iceberg**: Only a small visible part of a much larger problem or situation. 
    > 冰山一角（巨大问题或情况中仅露出的一小部分）
* **Mechanical logic**: Using the way things physically work to explain an idea. 
    > 机械逻辑（利用物理运作方式来解释概念）
* **Cognitive load**: The total amount of mental effort being used in the working memory. 
    > 认知负荷（工作记忆中使用的心理努力总量）
* **Affective filter**: An emotional barrier that prevents learning (like stress or fear). 
    > 情感过滤（阻碍学习的情感障碍，如压力或恐惧）
* **Empathy gap**: The lack of understanding between two different experiences. 
    > 共情鸿沟（不同经历之间缺乏理解的情况）
* **Internal translation lag**: The delay caused by translating English in one's head. 
    > 内部翻译延迟（在大脑中先用母语翻译英语导致的延迟）
* **Hyper-specific**: Extremely detailed and precise. 
    > 高度特定（极其详尽且精准）
* **Actionable takeaway**: A practical piece of advice that can be used immediately. 
    > 可操作的要点（可以立即使用的实用建议）
* **List fatigue**: Getting tired from seeing too many items or tips at once. 
    > 清单疲劳（一次看到太多项目或技巧而感到疲惫）
