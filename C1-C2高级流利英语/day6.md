### 核心动词 + 高频短语 速记清单

**核心动词 (Core Verbs)**
* **Walk / Walked**: To move at a regular pace by lifting and setting down each foot in turn.
    > 走 / 走了（规则动词，戴上“制服帽子”）
* **Watch / Watched**: To look at or observe attentively over a period of time.
    > 看 / 看了（规则动词，低摩擦信号）
* **Go / Went**: To move from one place to another.
    > 去 / 去了（“卧底叛逆”动词，完全改变身份）
* **Have / Had**: To possess, own, or hold.
    > 有 / 有了（不规则动词，形态结构塌陷）
* **See / Saw**: To perceive with the eyes.
    > 看见 / 看见了（高频动词，抵制标准化）
* **Swim / Swam**: To propel oneself through water.
    > 游泳 / 游了泳（动力性动作动词）

**高频短语 (High-Frequency Phrases)**
* **Yesterday**: On the day before today.
    > 昨天（强力语义覆盖，救援交流）
* **Last week**: During the week preceding the current one.
    > 上周（时间锚点，建立坐标）
* **Two hours ago**: At a time two hours before the present.
    > 两小时前（代数模块化短语，坐标精准定位）
* **Did you...?**: Used to start a question about the past.
    > 你当时……了吗？（“时光机大门”，释放认知压力）
* **Was / Were happy**: A state of feeling or showing pleasure or contentment.
    > 过去很快乐（环境底色，赋予叙事温度）
* **Affective filter**: A psychological barrier that can inhibit language acquisition.
    > 情感过滤（降低戒备，让语言更友好）
